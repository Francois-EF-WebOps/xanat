# KIT.md — Cultivation Claim and Pilot Kit

**Status:** verify the current repository scope before planning any physical cultivation. The README contains a claim about orchid cultivation and hand-pollination; treat it as a claim requiring source verification, not an automatically established fact.

## Read first
- `README.md`
- existing research, species names, locations, methods and citations
- any relevant conservation, biosecurity and plant-trade restrictions

## Requirements artefacts
For each species or cultivation claim record accepted scientific name, local name if sourced, native/range status, source and precise location, growth requirements, pollination evidence, legal status, risks of collection/transport, and confidence level.

## Low-cost procurement
Do not purchase plants, seeds, pollinators, pesticides or specialist equipment until species identity, legality and biosecurity have been checked. First inventory existing tools and use open primary or institutional sources. For any lawful pilot, document item, specification, quantity, source/date, cost, alternatives, water/light/space needs and disposal plan.

## Procedure
1. Verify the species and the claim from reliable botanical or institutional sources.
2. Check whether the organism is protected, invasive, trade-restricted or subject to biosecurity controls.
3. Define a small, non-destructive observation before attempting cultivation.
4. Record environmental conditions, materials and methods.
5. Avoid collecting wild organisms or moving plants/pollen between sites without required permission.
6. Record outcomes including failure, uncertainty and confounders.
7. Publish no claim of causation from a single observation.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Species identity and source are traceable.
- [ ] Legal and biosecurity checks are recorded.
- [ ] No unpermitted collection or transfer occurs.
- [ ] Requirements and BOM are specific and costed or marked unknown.
- [ ] Method and outcome can be repeated.
- [ ] Claims do not exceed the evidence.
